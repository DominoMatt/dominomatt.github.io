# The Lamplit Boneyard

The accent palette for the site's ten games. It stays a boneyard at its core —
muted, earthy, well-worn tiles — but the warm end carries the light, like a
candlelit tavern table where the games actually get played: dark wood,
flame-yellow, ale, slate, and the muted greens and blues of well-used felt.

## Two registers

- **Warm / lit** — `#f0ce49` `#E07B39` `#8f3327` `#98683a` `#b171ae`
- **Cool / grounded** — `#6d5a93` `#3f7d74` `#4a6147` `#436189` `#8a8a8a`
