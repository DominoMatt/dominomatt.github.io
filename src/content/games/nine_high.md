### Warning: This game is untested. Maybe we can give it a try at Comic Con.

- Shed your hand by playing one or more dominoes (as equal end matched 'sets' of dominoes) to the play area that outranks the current highest 'set' of dominoes played. (see Rankings below)
- Sets' are groups of dominoes that all share a common end value (are equal end matched).

## Setup:

- Shuffle/Mix all the dominoes face-down and
- Deal 9 dominoes to each player as their hand.
- Choose a 1st player, they play the initial tile of the hand. (1st player rotates between hands)

## Gameplay:

- Each round progresses over a number of plays, each play must outrank the previously high play.

#### On your turn:

1. Play or Pass: A) Play 1 or more tiles to the play area that outranks (see Rankings below) the current highest 'set'. B) Pass , if you pass you can't play on a future turn for rest of the round.
2. Continue taking turns in order (skipping players that have passed), until all players have passed.

When all players have passed the round is over. The current player own the high play discards it and plays the strting tile in the following round.

If a player shed their hand the Hand is done.

### Play

If Playing, do 1 and then 2...

1. Play 1 or more dominoes, following both these rules:
   - The value of your play must exceed the value (see Rankings below) of the current play.
   - The number of dominoes you may play on your turn, to beat the current play, is either:
     <ol type="a">
     <li>the same number of dominoes, or</li>
     <li>1 more than the current number dominoes already in play.</li>
     </ol>
2. When you make a play; you must take 1 of the dominoes from the previous play, the remaining tiles are then discarded from the area of play.
   - You can't pick up if it's the 1st play in the round.
   - You also don't pick up a tile if it's the last play of the hand, i.e. someone shed their entire hand.

### Pass

- Pass your turn, if you pass you can't play on a future turn for rest of the round
- If all other players have passed their turn and it's the player's turn again who made the last play, the round ends, that player winning the round.

## Ranking

- A larger set will win.
- If the set is the same size see Values ranking.

### Values ranking

Rankings of sets: 0/blank, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7 ,8, 9, doubles (Lowest to Highest)

## Score

When a hand is complete because a player went out/shed their hand (has 0 dominoes in hand). Each player adds a point to their score for each domino left in their hand. When a player score exceeds 10 points the game ends... **low score wins**.
