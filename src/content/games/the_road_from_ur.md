## Gameplay Overview

### Block Dominoes: Laying the way

Phase 1: A [block](https://dominomatt.github.io/notes/block_dominoes/) domino game

- All dominoes left in player's hand when the round is done are carried forward into Phase 2.

| Players | Hand size |
| ------- | --------- |
| 2       | 9         |
| 3       | 8         |
| 4       | 6         |

### Roll and Move: Running the road

Phase 2: On your turn... Roll 2d6 and move your pawns twice, one for each dice.

- Each domino is a space for the roll and move phase .
- Each domino has 2 ends. One pawn can sit on each end, thus 2 pawns may share the same domino (space).

| Players | Pawns |
| ------- | ----- |
| 2       | 4     |
| 3       | 3     |
| 4       | 3     |

## Running the Road: Roll and Move

- Can be played with opposed movement or same direction of movement.
- The player with the most tiles left over from phase 1 chooses the which end they start from.

#### On Your Turn

- Roll 2d6 and move twice.
- You must move each of the dice values (not just their sum). If a 2 and 5 are rolled, you must move a pawn 2 spaces and a pawn 5 spaces, in either order.

### Movement Rules:

- When you land on a space (domino) and there is an open end... land there.
- When you land on a space (domino) and both ends are already taken:

  1. There is 2 of your own pawns there... move either 1 space backward or forward.
  2. There is 2 of your opponents pawns there... move 1 space backward.
  3. There is 1 of yours and 1 opponent pawn there... send your opponent back to their start.

- When you move off of a domino and you are the last pawn on it (last pawn off)... take that tile (unless it's a double) and the layout is pushed together and shortened.

### Doubles ae Safe

- Doubles are considered 'safe' and were played 90 degrees to the layout in phase 1.
- Doubles can never be removed from the layout , last pawn off a double leaves it in place ('a safe space').
