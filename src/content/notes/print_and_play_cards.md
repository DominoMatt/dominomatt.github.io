Does 'printing' something make it print and play (PNP)?

- A game component that you actually play the game with or on is 'print and play'.
- Whereas a printable rule sheet does not make something print and play.

## Cards as Print and Play

I think cards almost always fall under the category of print and play.

- Afterall, why is it you'd print a sheet of cards... to play with them.
- On the other hand I suppose they could fall in realm of 'trading' cards.
