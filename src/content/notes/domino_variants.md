This is a central repo for all the traditional domino games that are here on the site.

## Basic Dominoes

- Basic Dominoes is the most simple version of dominoes, it is played with a double six set.
- Basic Dominoes is a blocking game (a shedding game) that uses equal end matching to form a line layout.
- The line of play has two open ends, one or both maybe become blocked in play.
- Doubles can be set 90 degrees to layout for 'style', this doesn't mean they are spinners.

### Draw

When you can't play, draw 1 or more dominoes from the boneyard by either...

<ol type="a">
    <li>Draw tiles from the boneyard until you can make a play. OR</li>
    <li>Draw 1 tile and then either make a play or pass (I like this one).</li>  
</ol>

### Block

After drawing your initial hands, set the boneyard aside... no more dominoes will be drawn during gameplay.

- If you don't have a play pass your turn, hopefully being able to resume play on your next turn.

## Cross Dominoes

- Cross dominoes can be played with either a double 6 or 9 set, depending on player count.
- It is best played with 3-5 players with a set double 9 dominoes.
- A Cross Domino layout has 4 open ends.
- In Cross Dominoes, you make the first double set to the layout a four way spinner.
- The doubles set beyond the 1st are not spinners and don't branch the layout.
